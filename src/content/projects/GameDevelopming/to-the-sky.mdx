## The first Unity game I released

ToTheSky was my first Unity project and my first game for a phone. I spent roughly three to four months learning the engine and C#, drawing textures, and building the game with documentation and tutorials. It reached Google Play on 20 February 2022. The listing is no longer available.

The idea is a star trying to return to the sky. The player steers horizontally, avoids clouds, and collects coins while the endless run gradually speeds up. The best score is saved between attempts.

<Image index={1} group open />

## Three lives in a moving sky

The settings offered two control schemes: following a finger or using the left and right halves of the screen as buttons. A cloud collision removes one of three lives. Leaving the screen also costs a life and returns the star to the centre.

The star, camera, and background stay in place; obstacles and collectables travel downwards. I created objects with Instantiate and removed them with Destroy behind the camera. Clouds appeared either at the star’s current horizontal position or randomly, leaving space to move aside.

Difficulty rises through an overall increase in speed. The bonus box temporarily accelerates time, making clouds, coins, and the rest of the gameplay move faster.

## Coins, skins, and a different-looking star

Collected coins could be spent on star skins with their own appearance and particles. The shop and its colour controls gave the small endless game a layer of personalisation beyond the current run.

<Gallery indices={[3, 4]} group open />

## Learning C# through a complete small game

After Blueprint, C# felt unfamiliar. Saving progress, managing skins, keeping counters, and setting up random spawning and increasing difficulty were all new tasks. The interface was the part I found easiest; the rest had to be learned as I went.

The Unlock Secret screen rewarded players for entering special codes I issued. Each code granted a set number of coins. The surviving editor screenshot records work on the reward animation.

## 2023 · a sequel explored in 3D

In October 2023 I returned to the idea with more experience and started reworking it in 3D. The old short video combines footage of the first game with modelling for this new attempt. That version was stopped in 2023.

I wanted to rewrite the systems so they would be easier to extend, improve the presentation, and add more skins, mechanics, levels, and modes instead of relying on one endless run. These were goals for the sequel, rather than features of a completed release.

<Image index={9} group open />

## 2024 · a new 2D interface

I returned again in 2024, starting the sequel over in 2D. I redrew the interface and worked on its animations. In a later revision, Play opened a menu for selecting a mode and its level. The menu flow worked, but the actual gameplay was not completed.

The cloud shader was used in the menu and configured further in the game scene. Its separate page shows the effect and its controls. Together, the two games mark my first finished mobile release and a later attempt to apply what I had learned to its visuals and structure.
